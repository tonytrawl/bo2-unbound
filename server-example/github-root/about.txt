ABOUT BLACK OPS 2 UNBOUND HQ

Black Ops 2 Unbound HQ is a Wii U homebrew application for installing and updating the
Black Ops 2 Unbound project. Its current Workshop installs curated Aroma plugins; community mods,
comments, ratings, and uploads are planned for a future release.

The application detects the installed game and update title IDs, storage locations, and active
language before writing files. One universal package is routed into the registered update title and
its correct localized content directory. Downloads are versioned and verified with SHA-256.

Black Ops 2 Unbound is a community project and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Activision,
Treyarch, or Pretendo Network.
