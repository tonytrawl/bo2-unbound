## Grind and Project KENNEL Have Arrived (now with Peacekeeper) 

![The Seagulls messed up the peacekeeper rollout not me...](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tonytrawl/bo2-unbound/refs/heads/main/Grindbeta.png)

### Included in this release

- **Grind MP map** — Take the fight to the legendary skate park.
- **Project KENNEL** — Survive a containment zone overrun by weaponized attack dogs.
- **Mod loader** — Load and manage mods both in the HQ and ingame.
- **FOV slider** — Adjust your field of view to fit the way you play.
- **Sample Mods** — Some Mods to get you started including a port of **Chaos Mod by JF**
---

## Project KENNEL

**Project KENNEL has lost control.** Across the quarantine zone, military attack dogs obey a corrupted command: *hunt until nothing remains.* Their handlers are gone, their safeguards have failed, and the kennels are still releasing them.

Fight the hordes with up to **four soldiers**. Scavenge supplies, upgrade your weapons, and keep your squad alive against relentless packs, burning explosives, and electrically charged predators.

*Hold out for extraction before the containment strike erases every trace of the program—including you.*
