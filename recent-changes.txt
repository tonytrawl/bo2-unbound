# BETA 3: DIE RISE IS HERE!
![Black Ops II Unbound - Beta 3](https://github.com/tonytrawl/bo2-unbound/blob/main/banners/beta3.png)

Beta 3 has officially arrived, bringing **Die Rise** to Unbound

This update introduces another fan-favorite Zombies map, alongside some much-needed improvements and fixes for our existing multiplayer maps.

**Game-content release: 0.2.0.** Open **Install / Update** in HQ to get the full release.

---

## Die Rise

The skyscrapers are waiting! Fight your way through the undead, navigate the rooftops, and do your best not to take a wrong step. The zombies aren't the only thing trying to kill you on this map.

### Known issue: explosion crashes

Character dialogue has not yet been added to Die Rise. We're working on getting it implemented, with a **patch next week**.

The good news? Thanks to our update system, you won't have to download the entire map again!

Once released, the patch should take approximately 3 minutes to download and install, depending on your connection and storage.
---

## Mirage & Grind - Multiplayer

**Mirage** gets its full audio with ambience sounds. **Grind** no longer has the previously reported graphical issues, but map ambience sounds still need to be added.
