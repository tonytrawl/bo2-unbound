# BETA 2: NUKETOWN, MIRAGE & MORE

![Black Ops II Unbound - Beta 2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tonytrawl/bo2-unbound/main/banners/beta2.png)

Beta 2 brings **Nuketown Zombies** and **Mirage Multiplayer** to Unbound, alongside a **beta Server Browser** and **native keyboard and mouse support**.

**Game-content release: 0.1.0.** Open **Install / Update** in HQ to get the full release.

---

## Nuketown Zombies

Fight to survive on Nuketown in Zombies. The map is available for testing, with **up to four-player local split-screen**.

- Enable the **Unbound Weapons** lobby toggle to add the **Wunderwaffe DG-2** and **Thundergun** to the Mystery Box.
- The **Ray Gun Mark II** is also available in the Mystery Box on all Zombies maps.

### Known issue: explosion crashes

Some explosions can still cause crashes due to physics interactions. A Nuketown fix is planned as a **patch next week**.

Once available, the patch is expected to take approximately **3-5 minutes to download and install**, depending on your connection and storage. You will **not need another huge full download** for that fix.

---

## Mirage & Grind - Multiplayer

**Mirage** joins the Multiplayer map lineup. **Grind has been fixed** and no longer has the previously reported issues.

---

## Server Browser - Beta

Browse available **Public Match playlists** from the online lobby. **Find Match** remains available if you prefer the usual matchmaking flow.

You can also **host custom server matches** for others to find through Public Match search or the Server Browser.

### Known issue: failed joins

The Server Browser is in **beta**. Rarely, joining a match may show **"Match unjoinable"** or freeze your console if the join fails. A failed join does **not mean your Unbound installation is damaged**.

---

## Native Keyboard & Mouse

Use a **USB keyboard and mouse for player one**, including mouse aiming, firing, movement, and gameplay actions.

- Use the **mouse cursor in TV menus**.
- Adjust your settings under **Options > Keyboard & Mouse**.

## Developer Console

Press **~ (tilde)** while using a keyboard to open the console. **Keyboard text entry** and **Backspace** are supported.

---

## PROJECT KENNEL 2.0

*The gates are open. The hunt continues.*

New weapons, characters, animations, power-ups, music, and gameplay improvements arrive in this update.

- The **Ray Gun Mark II** joins the Mystery Cache, with an upgraded **Pack-a-Punch** version.
- Power-ups use their **original models and announcer calls**.
- Power-up benefits are **shared with the squad**.

### Easter Egg - all maps

Find the **four glowing radios** on any map to uncover the KENNEL story, featuring **voice acting from the Unbound team**.

Complete the hunt to unlock a **surprise weapon** and an **Easter Egg song**.

*The radios are waiting. Happy hunting!*

---

## Unbound Mod Loader

- Switch mods more easily **from the in-game menu**.
- See **which mod is currently loaded**.
- Get **clearer loading errors** when something goes wrong.
- Record and play back **Theater footage locally from an SD card**.

## Unbound T6 Mod Tool - Public 2.0

The separate **PC Mod Tool** adds a redesigned workspace, optional **GSC, CSC, and Lua syntax checks**, and improved **PC mod conversion and file handling**.

*The PC Mod Tool is a separate download from the Unbound GitHub repository. HQ does not install it on your console.*

---

### Install Beta 2

Return to **Install / Update** in HQ to download **0.1.0**. This is a **full release**, so new and existing players can install it directly without downloading the older releases first.
