# BETA 1.5: NUKETOWN IS HERE

![Nuketown Zombies and PROJECT KENNEL 2.0 - Unbound Beta 1.5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tonytrawl/bo2-unbound/main/banners/1.5banner.png)

**Nuketown Zombies** joins Unbound. Explore the **PROJECT KENNEL** Easter egg, try the updated in-game Mod Loader, and check out the new PC Mod Tool.

---

## Nuketown Zombies

Bring the fight to Nuketown in Zombies. Explore the map, take on the undead, and see how long you can survive.

- Play local split-screen Zombies with **up to four players**.
- Enable the **Wunderwaffe DG-2** and **Thundergun** in the Mystery Box with the **Unbound Weapons** lobby toggle.
- Find the **Ray Gun Mark II** in Mystery Boxes across all supported Zombies maps.

### Known issues

**Please read before playing:** Some textures and environmental effects may look incorrect. Physics interactions can cause crashes, including when using the **Thundergun**.

---

## PROJECT KENNEL 2.0

*The gates are open. The hunt continues.*

KENNEL gets new weapons, characters, animations, power-ups, Easter eggs, and gameplay improvements.

- **Ray Gun Mark II** added to the Mystery Cache, with an upgraded Pack-a-Punch version.
- **CDC survivor model**, perk-drinking animations, and an authentic Pack-a-Punch machine.
- Original **Max Ammo** and **Double Points** models, effects, and announcer calls. Power-up benefits are shared with the squad.
- More dog entrances and behaviors, custom music, refined lighting, an updated HUD, and improved extraction presentation.
- Fixes and polish for weapons, vendors, revives, scoring, and more.

### The KENNEL Easter egg

Listen closely for the **four glowing radios** hidden around the map. Find them all to uncover the KENNEL story, brought to life with **voice acting from the Unbound team**.

Complete the hunt to unlock a **surprise weapon** and an **Easter Egg song**.

*Available on every PROJECT KENNEL map. The radios are waiting. Happy hunting!*

## Mod Loader Update

Modding gets easier to manage, with performance improvements and a clearer in-game workflow.

- Switch mods or unload the active mod from the **Mods** menu.
- See which mod is currently loaded.
- Access the menu from supported Multiplayer and Zombies lobbies.
- Get clearer errors when a mod cannot load.
- Keep your mod selection when returning from a match.
- Record and play back **Theater footage locally from an SD card**.

---

## Unbound T6 Mod Tool: Public 2.0

The separate **PC modding tool** has a redesigned workspace for creating, editing, and converting Black Ops II Wii U mods.

- Optional syntax checks for **GSC, CSC, and Lua**.
- Convert supported files from unpacked PC mods, with progress and cancellation.
- Safer batch conversion, filename-collision checks, and prompts before replacing files.
- Save, discard, or cancel before leaving unsaved edits.
- Remembered preferences, improved help, and a clearer workflow.

**Conversion limits:** PC conversion does not resolve PC-only functions or missing dependencies. Converted mods still need in-game testing. The public edition supports **loose mods only** and does **not include FF building**.

*The PC tool is a separate download from the Unbound GitHub repository. HQ does not install it on your console.*
