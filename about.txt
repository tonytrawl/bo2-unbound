ABOUT BLACK OPS 2 UNBOUND HQ

Black Ops 2 Unbound HQ is a Wii U homebrew application for installing and updating the
Black Ops 2 Unbound project and browsing curated community Workshop content.

The application detects the installed regional game, update, AOC, and storage location before
writing files. Downloads are versioned and verified with SHA-256 before installation.

Black Ops 2 Unbound is a community project and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Activision,
Treyarch, or Pretendo Network.
